# Wise Design System - Key Reference

## Colors
- primary (lime-green): #9fe870
- on-primary (text on green): #0e0f0c
- primary-active (hover): #cdffad
- primary-pale (light bg): #e2f6d5
- ink (near-black): #0e0f0c
- ink-deep: #163300
- body (secondary text): #454745
- mute (caption): #868685
- canvas (white): #ffffff
- canvas-soft (sage bg): #e8ebe6
- positive: #2ead4b
- negative: #d03238
- warning: #ffd11a
- accent-orange: #ffc091
- accent-cyan: #38c8ff

## Typography
- Display: Inter weight 900 (substitute for Wise Sans)
- Sub-display: Inter weight 600
- Body: Inter weight 400
- Sizes: mega 126px, xxl 96px, xl 64px, lg 47px, md 40px, sm 32px, xs 24px
- Body: lg 20px, md 16px, sm 14px, caption 12px

## Border Radius
- xl (canonical): 24px for buttons and cards
- md: 12px for inputs
- pill: 9999px for badges

## Key Principles
- Lime-green CTA is the ONLY accent color
- Weight 900 for hero headlines, 600 for everything else
- Sage canvas-soft (#e8ebe6) as hero background, white cards on top
- Surface contrast = elevation (no shadows)
- 24px rounded corners on all cards and buttons
- Dark footer with ink background
- Hero band: sage bg + massive 900-weight headline
